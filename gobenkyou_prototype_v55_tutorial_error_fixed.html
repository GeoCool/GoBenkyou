Quick guide

How to use the practice

Each practice gives you a sentence in your native language. Your job is to break it down using GoBenkyou's position system.

  1. Think through the sentence. Assign each word its position in your mind.
  2. Recall the meanings. Think of each word’s meaning in your mind; you do not need to type answers.
  3. Reveal help when needed. Scroll down to see the full vertical breakdown, then use the bottom button to move on whenever you feel ready.

You can scroll up and down through the practice. Skip this guide to start right away.
